Breadth-First Search

BFS explores a graph outward from a start vertex one layer at a time: a vertex is undiscovered until found, then discovered and placed on a queue, and finally processed once all its edges have been examined. Because the queue is FIFO, every vertex at distance d from the start is processed before any vertex at distance d+1 — which is exactly why BFS also computes shortest paths when every edge has the same weight.
