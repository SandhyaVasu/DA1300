Reversing a Singly Linked List

Reversal walks the chain once, rewiring each node's next pointer to point backward instead of forward — no new nodes, no extra storage. Three pointers do the work: prev trails behind, current is the node being rewired, and next_node saves the rest of the chain before the link is overwritten. When current runs off the end, head is moved to prev — the old last node.
