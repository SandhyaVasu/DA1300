Dijkstra's Two-Stack Algorithm

A fully parenthesised arithmetic expression can be evaluated with two stacks and one left-to-right scan. Numbers go on the operand stack, symbols go on the operator stack, and ( is skipped. Every ) fires a reduction: pop an operator and its two operands, apply the operator, and push the result back as a new operand.
