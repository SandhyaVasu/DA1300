Dijkstra's Shortest Path Algorithm

Dijkstra's algorithm grows a cloud of vertices outward from the source, always admitting next the vertex outside the cloud with the smallest tentative distance — a greedy, weighted generalization of BFS. Admitting a vertex u triggers edge relaxation on each of its edges: if going through u gives a shorter path to a neighbour v, dist[v] and pred[v] are updated.
